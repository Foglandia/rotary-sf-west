Learn about and experience Greek culture without having to fly to Greece. Join us at the Oakland Greek Festival to enjoy Greek customs, food, wine, music and dance.  Past District 5150 Governor and Past Club President Peter Lagarias will be the host and guide for this event.  Come and learn the Zorba dance, listen to some Greek history, and tour the Greek Orthodox Cathedral of the Ascension with fellow Rotarians and friends.  Bring your appetite for delicious Mediterranean food.
